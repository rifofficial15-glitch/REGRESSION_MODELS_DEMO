JUST AN EXAMPLE FOR AB TEST IN MACHINE LEARNING.

HERE WE ARE PERFORMING AB TEST TO FIND OUT WHETHER THERE IS ANY RELATIONSHIP BETWEEN PURCHASING RATE AND RECOMMENDATION TYPE..

WE WANT TO KNOW WHETHER THE NUMBER OF CUSTOMERS PURCHASE THE SCREENGUARD ALONG WITH PHONE IS HIGHER OR THE NUMBER WHO PURCHASE IT ALONG WITH COVER..

NULL HYPOTHESIS = "THERE IS NO RELATIONSHIP B/W PURCHASE RATE & RECOMMENDATION TYPE. THEY ARE INDEPENDENT" ALTERNATE HYPOTHESIS = " THERE IS RELATIONSHIP B/W PURCHASE RATE & RECOMMENDATION TYPE. THEY ARE NOT INDEPENDENT"
